# Pebble Beach Tour d’Elegance reroute example — August 2026

Use as a pattern for converting an official event reroute map into spectator logistics.

## Official facts found

- Official update page: `https://www.pebblebeachconcours.net/updates/`
- Official route PDF: `https://www.pebblebeachconcours.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-Concours-Tour-Map-8-11-26-web.pdf`
- Update stated the traditional Big Sur / Highway 1 route was modified due to the Timber Fire and corresponding evacuations.
- Revised Tour remains in Pebble Beach and Monterey.
- Start/finish remained on Portola Road near Concours Village, not far from The Lodge at Pebble Beach.
- Lineup: 7:00am.
- Departure waves: 9:30, 9:45, 10:00.

## Planning lessons

- Explicitly flag event-date mismatch: Tour d’Elegance route map was Thursday Aug 13; Concours Sunday ticket is a separate Sunday event.
- Rank reliability over scenery when closures/reroutes are involved.
- Best high-certainty recommendation: start/finish near Portola Road / Concours Village.
- Best scenic moving-car options if accessible: 17-Mile Drive coastal turnouts such as Bird Rock Vista Point, Fanshell Beach, Seal Rock, Cypress Point Lookout, Pescadero Point.
- Lower-crowd interior alternatives: Shepherd’s Knoll / Forest Lake Road / Huckleberry Hill zone.
- Monterey/Ryan Ranch side can be a fallback if Pebble Beach access gets congested or gated.
- Warn user not to chase the route; pick one watch spot plus one fallback.
