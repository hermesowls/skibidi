# Kian Trading Communication

## User preferences
- Social posts for average stock traders should be concise and plain-language: bias, My Trade Opinion, nearby hold/reclaim/loss levels, targets, invalidation. Avoid exhaustive node inventories and Markdown asterisks.
- Distinguish early speculative positioning from later confirmation. The user objects to treating overhead targets as the only acceptable entry: options can appreciate before the underlying reaches that level. A target can be a profit-management checkpoint. Early positioning still requires an observed base/rejection and defined risk; heatmaps alone cannot confirm support.
- For completed trades, explain the retrospective setup rather than giving a new-entry recommendation. Separate user-reported profits from verified evidence; do not invent subsequent price action or claim nodes caused returns.

## Lessons from corrections
- A GOOGL bullish-bounce interpretation was challenged after a loss. Positive exposure below spot is not confirmed support or sufficient evidence of bullish direction; it can be a lower pin/destination. Without price confirmation, use neutral/conditional language.
- Assuming actual dealer net gamma, positive gamma hedging tends to sell rises and buy declines; negative gamma tends to buy rises and sell declines. Negative nodes are not automatically resistance. Provider sign conventions and positioning assumptions matter.
- VEX describes delta sensitivity to implied volatility. Do not apply GEX hedging rules directly or portray positive VEX as a guaranteed upward magnet.
- Distant King Nodes provide conditional context, not guaranteed destinations. Distinguish expirations, chart evidence, and speculative mechanics.
